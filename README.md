# 3D Space Shooter (Hand Tracking)

A modular Pygame space shooter featuring real-time hand-gesture controls powered by OpenCV and MediaPipe. Drive your ship with hand movement, destroy procedural 3D asteroids, and defeat the Final Boss.

## Features
* **Hand Controls:** Move with your index finger; pinch to shoot (includes mouse fallback).
* **3D Visuals:** Procedural 3D asteroid meshes with real-time lighting and rotation physics.
* **Boss Fight:** Final boss battle featuring dynamic health bars and spread attacks.
* **Modular Code:** Separated into `spaceship.py`, `boss.py`, `asteroid.py`, and `main.py`.
